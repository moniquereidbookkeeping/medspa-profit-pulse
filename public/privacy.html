Privacy

The MedSpa Profit Pulse, a free tool by Monique Reid Bookkeeping. Last updated October 26, 2026.

This page explains in plain language what the calculator saves and what happens if you ask for a free review.

If you only use the calculator

The numbers you type stay in your own browser so they are still there next time you visit. They are not sent to us. The "Reset Defaults" button clears them. Printing a report is free and needs no sign-up.

If you request a free review

When you press "Request My Free Review", the following is sent to Monique Reid Bookkeeping:

  • Your name, practice name and email address, and your phone number if you chose to give it.
  • Anything you typed in the optional feedback box.
  • The calculator entries behind your result (treatment type, payment platform, price, costs and percentages) and the result itself.
  • The time you agreed to be contacted.

Please do not enter patient names or any health information. The calculator does not need them.

Why we use it

To prepare and follow up on your review, and to tell you about our bookkeeping services, as you agreed when you ticked the box. You can say no and still use the calculator and print your report.

Where it is kept

Your request is stored in a Google Sheet that Monique Reid Bookkeeping controls. This site is hosted on Cloudflare. When it is switched on, a Cloudflare security check confirms that you are a person and not an automated program. We may also count visits to this page without recording who you are, using Cloudflare Web Analytics or Plausible.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you, to show you what we hold about you, or to delete it. Contact us through moniquereidbookkeeping.com and we will act on your request.

A note on the numbers

All results are estimates based on what you enter, for planning and discussion with your accountant. They are not tax, legal or accounting advice.
