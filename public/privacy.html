Privacy

The Profit Pulse, a free tool by Monique Reid Bookkeeping. Last updated October 27, 2026.

This page explains in plain language what the calculator saves and what happens if you ask for a free review.

If you only use the calculator

The numbers you type stay in your own browser so they are still there next time you visit. They are not sent to us. The "Reset Defaults" button clears them. Results on the screen are free and need no sign-up. The full printable report is unlocked when you share your contact details (see below).

If you unlock your full report

When you press "Unlock My Full Report", the following is sent to Monique Reid Bookkeeping:

  • Your name, practice name and email address, and your phone number if you chose to give it.
  • Anything you typed in the optional feedback box.
  • The calculator entries behind your result (treatment type, payment platform, price, costs and percentages) and the result itself.
  • The time you agreed to be contacted.

Please do not enter patient names or any health information. The calculator does not need them.

Why we use it

To give you your full report, to prepare and follow up on your free review, and to tell you about our bookkeeping services, as you agreed when you ticked the box. You can say no and still use the calculator and see your results on the screen.

Where it is kept

Your request is saved in our private business records, stored with trusted providers (Google for storage and Cloudflare for hosting and security). A Cloudflare security check confirms you are a person and not an automated program. We also count visits to this page without recording who you are, using Cloudflare Web Analytics. We keep your details only as long as we need them to follow up, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you, to show you what we hold about you, or to delete it. Contact us through moniquereidbookkeeping.com and we will act on your request.

A note on the numbers

All results are estimates based on what you enter, for planning and discussion with your accountant. They are not tax, legal or accounting advice.
